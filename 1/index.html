Kaladin Stormblessed

Basic Character Information:

Kaladin Stormblessed is one of the main protagonists from Brandon Sanderson's high fantasy book series The Stormlight Archive. He started out as a surgeon, became a soldier to save his brother Tien, and was then forced into slavery in the bridge crews, culminating in his suicidal state at his introduction. However, thanks to the bonds he forms in Bridge Four and his new radiant powers, he is able to free himself and his comrades from damnation allowing them to find a better life. Kaladin helps train an Order of the ancient Knights Radiant called the Windrunners in order to stop the end of all mankind.

My three reasons why I like Kaladin:

  • He is very cunning, often able to get the upperhand on his opponents when he is usually outnumbered.
  • He treats others with respect, even when they are his adversary.
  • He takes care of the people most important to him and would do anything to prevent them from harm.
