Flute

Flutes are classified as edge blown aerophones. They make sound by blowing air across the mouthpiece hole, causing the air inside the tube to vibrate. The pitch of the note is determined by keys pressed down which opens and closes holes along the body of the instrument.

Classical Applications

Flutes are very expressive with their vocal and bright sound. They blend well with many instruments and typically sustain the melody of the piece. Typically, flutes are used to create a feeling of whimsy, magic, and love.

Famous Piece

My Heart Will Go On from Titanic is a piece that symbolizes the meaning of star-crossed lovers. The piece opens with a flute solo that is both saddening, heartfelt, longing. Considering the viewer already knows how the story will end, it makes the somber attitude that much greater.

Fun Fact!

Due to the nature of flutes, they have been made out of many materials. To note some of the distinctive examples, there are flutes made of bone, ivory, glass, jade, and even gold.
