Web Application Proposal

Topic Description

The topic I have chosen is the orchestra and music theory. An orchestra is a collection of musicians that typically include strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion. Orchestras are unique from wind ensembles and marching bands as they contain the widest range of instruments to choose from. In addition, I want to cover some of the basic aspects of music theory. Music theory is the study of how music is made. It involves learning chords, rhythms, instrumentation, and more in order to combine them into a piece of music.

This topic is of particular interest to me since I am earning a music minor. I have also played in many campus ensembles on campus including the marching band, wind ensemble, and the LOU symphony orchestra. I believe my web application will be helpful as I want to also explore some of the more unique instruments in the orchestra. The more people know of these unique and interesting instruments, the more they will be able to appreciate them when attending concerts or listening to music in general.

A website that attempts to solve this problem is the Spartanburg Philharmonic website. This is one of the first websites that come up when searching for 'the instruments of the orchestra'. This website contains little information on each instrument it lists (as well as only covering very basic staples). I also found several broken links towards the end of the page. Another website is the Philharmonia website. This website is much better with more information and better formatting. I plan to make my website more akin to this one with a more varied pool of instruments.

App Description

The app will have these pages:

  1. Home Page

    In the home page, I plan to introduce the orchestra to the user. I will answer questions such as what is an orchestra, what makes it different from other instrumental ensembles, and more. There will be images that show orchestras and concert halls. I also plan to add sound bites from a few famous orchestra pieces in order to acquaint the user with the sounds of the orchestra.

  2. Two-Step Page

    The structure for this page will look something like this:

    • Strings
      • Violin
      • Viola
      • Cello
      • Double Bass
    • Woodwinds
      • Flute
      • Oboe
      • Clarinet
      • Saxophone
    • Brass
      • Trumpet
      • French Horn
      • Trombone
      • Tuba
    • Percussion
      • Timpani
      • Marimba
      • Xylophone
      • Cymbals

    The basic syntax is that your first choice will be a group/category of instruments and your second choice will be an instrument in that category. However, this current list is subject to change as I plan to do more research and incorporate more unique instruments and possibly even different groups/categories.

    The final information for each instrument will include an in-depth description of the instrument, how it is played, its classical applications, and some famous pieces featuring it. There will also be 2-3 pictures of the instrument/someone playing the instrument and a sound bite of the instrument that the user can listen to.

  3. Static Info Page

    This page will focus more on the music theory side of things instead of the instrumentation side. A little bit of basic information on chords and scales, various systems of music from around the world, and more information on the categorization of instruments from a classical perspective.

  4. Contact Us Page

    I intend to collect from the user their email (for contact), their favorite page, their least favorite page, and what topic/area they believe should be added to the website. There will also be a general message field for any information that may not be covered by the other fields.
